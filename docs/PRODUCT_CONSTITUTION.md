# DUE Product Constitution

## 1. Product Name
**DUE** — Stop chasing customers for money.

## 2. Core promise
DUE helps freelancers and small service businesses track money customers owe them and automate professional payment follow-up.

## 3. Core problem
Work is delivered before full payment. Owners manually track who owes what, when it was promised, overdue state, partial payments, and broken promises. Data is fragmented across WhatsApp, email, invoices, spreadsheets, bank alerts, notes, and memory.

## 4. Target user
Nigerian freelancers, agencies, and small service businesses (NGN, multi-tenant Business boundary).

## 5. Product thesis
Track receivables and automate professional payment follow-up without becoming a full accounting suite, CRM, or debt collector.

## 6. Non-goals (V1)
- General ledger / full accounting
- Inventory / POS
- Customer portal self-serve
- Legal collections / late fees automation
- WhatsApp / SMS (until authorized)
- Payment provider capture (Paystack/Flutterwave) as primary path
- AI copy generation
- Native mobile apps
- Multi-currency beyond NGN default

## 7. Money rules
- Integer minor units (kobo for NGN)
- Reject overpayments (no silent clamp, no credits in V1)
- Outstanding is computed, not stored as mutable truth alone

## 8. Authority order
1. This Product Constitution
2. Approved PRD
3. Architecture Decisions / ADRs
4. Current implementation plan
5. Individual development prompts
